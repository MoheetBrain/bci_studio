## 4b · The reconstructed neuron (H01 release) — added 2026-11-25

`public/world/h01-exemplar.json` is derived from one entry (`2252715458.0.swc`) of the "104 proofread cells" skeleton
archive of the H01 release (Google Research and the Lichtman Laboratory, Harvard University).

- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). The release's data page states that all released
  datasets are under this licence (page read 2026-11-25).
- **Attribution used:** Shapson-Coe A, Januszewski M, Berger DR, et al. *A petavoxel fragment of human cerebral
  cortex reconstructed at nanoscale resolution.* Science 384, eadk4858 (2024). doi:10.1126/science.adk4858. Source:
  https://h01-release.storage.googleapis.com/data.html
- **Changes made (CC BY requires saying so):** converted to micrometres and rotated into the cell's own frame; the
  nodes inside the cell body merged into one; each unbranched stretch simplified to within 0.18 µm; drawn as tubes.
  The file records the archive entry and its SHA-256.
- **Where the attribution appears:** in the file itself, on the neuron's card in the world ("Source, licence, what
  was changed"), in the "What is this?" panel (manifest entry `h01-exemplar`), in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and in
  `docs/EMBODIED.md` §5.
- **Not checked:** whether the dataset's authors ask for any wording beyond the citation; the paper itself was not
  re-read for this build (citation and abstract only). No endorsement by the authors is implied or claimed.
- Only this one entry (0.81 MB of a 59.8 MB archive) was downloaded. Neuroglancer was consulted for the data
  formats; none of its code is included.

## 5 · Open licence questions for a commercial release

1. **3D Slicer License, Part B** (read in full for this build). It grants a royalty-free licence to use, reproduce,
   make derivative works of, display and distribute, including incorporation into proprietary programs, provided that:
   (b) the licence terms appear in and apply to every copy, prefaced with the sentence "All or portions of this
   licensed product … have been obtained under license from The Brigham and Women's Hospital, Inc. …" — done in
   `public/atlas/LICENSE-3D-Slicer.txt` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and it must also appear in user documentation of
   any product; (c) attributions are preserved — done; (d) modified versions are clearly marked as modified — done in
   the licence file, the manifest, the atlas record of every structure and the provenance line on the home screen.
   Clause 4: the software and data are "designed for research purposes only", "clinical applications are neither
   recommended nor advised", and "any commercialization … is at the sole risk of the party" doing it. Clause 6: the
   names of Brigham, its affiliates, contributors and funders may not be used to endorse or promote a product without
   written permission. Counsel should confirm how clause 1(b) is to be met in a shipped product's documentation.
2. **Abstracts**: stay local. Do not ship them.
3. **A cytoarchitectonic M1** would need an atlas whose licence allows the intended use (Julich-Brain does not for
   commercial use).

- **DANDI 000019 attribution.** The decoder trace contains samples derived from this public dataset. The licence
  (CC-BY-4.0) and source are taken from `chip/docs/ML_EVALUATION.md`; the exact attribution wording required by the
  dataset’s own page has not been checked here and should be confirmed by the owner before any public release.
- **Tools used only to produce evidence, not shipped:** the Chrome installed on this machine (driven by
  `playwright-core`) and the `ffmpeg` installed on this machine (joins screencast frames into
  `docs/evidence/cinematic/interaction.mp4`). Nothing was downloaded for either.
